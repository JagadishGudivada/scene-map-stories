1. **Executive summary** — the verdict in a few lines: the market is bigger than ever, Sarevista is not late, but "a map of filming locations" is now crowded; the memory-map/passport layer is the defensible part.
2. **Market size and growth** — screen-inspired travel as of September 2026, with each figure labelled as an estimate and its source and date named.
3. **Audience sizing** — who they are, rough reachable share, and what that means for realistic early traffic.
4. **Competitor landscape** — SetJetters, Moveler, Cinemapper, SceneMap, SpotAtlas and generic "atlas" blogs: what each does well, where it stops, and how it makes money, plus a comparison table across the features that matter.
5. **Search demand reality check** — the keyword estimates with the market named, and what they imply (long-tail per-title and per-city pages, not the head term).
6. **Money model comparison** — free app vs paid app vs ads vs affiliate, and where Sarevista sits.
7. **Risks** — what would make this a bad bet, including the overlap between SetJetters' photo matching and points and Sarevista's Scene Mode and badges.
8. **Recommendation** — "late to market or not", the reasoning, and the first three moves.

Where evidence is missing, the report says so rather than filling the gap. No invented figures.
